AI tool sprawl is the fastest-growing category of unmanaged software spend. Based on Cledara platform data, 85% of companies now have dedicated AI subscriptions, with the average business paying for 4.5 AI tools. AI subscriptions grew 84% year over year, and nearly one in three new software purchases is now an AI tool.
