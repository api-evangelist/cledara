Jira dominates enterprise project management with deep customization, but Linear is winning engineering teams with speed and simplicity. Jira costs $7.91-$14.54/user/month with 3,000+ integrations and powerful reporting, while Linear starts at $8/user/month with a 2x faster interface and native GitHub integration. Engineering teams choosing between them face a trade-off: Jira for complex enterprises needing compliance and customization, Linear for teams prioritizing velocity and developer experience.
