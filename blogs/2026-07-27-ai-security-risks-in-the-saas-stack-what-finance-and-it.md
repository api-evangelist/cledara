AI tools now process a company's most sensitive data, from source code to financial models, yet most organisations lack the governance to manage the security risks that come with rapid AI adoption. Nearly 79% of companies on the Cledara platform pay for AI tools, with the average running 3.2 distinct AI subscriptions. IBM reports that 13% of organisations have experienced AI-related breaches, with 97% lacking proper access controls.
