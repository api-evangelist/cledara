SaaS management for finance teams goes beyond IT visibility. This guide covers the four core finance use cases, from budget forecasting and cost allocation to renewal management and audit readiness, with practical guidance on what CFOs should look for in a platform. Based on Cledara platform data showing companies manage an average of 35.8 software vendors, and industry research indicating 51% of SaaS licences go unused, the post makes the case that finance teams need purpose-built tools, not repurposed IT solutions.
