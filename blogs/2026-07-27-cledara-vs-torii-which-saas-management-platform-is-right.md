Cledara and Torii both help companies manage their SaaS stack, but they approach the problem from different angles. Torii is an IT-first platform built around shadow IT discovery, identity governance, and app lifecycle automation. Cledara is a finance-first platform that combines SaaS visibility with virtual card payments, accounting automation, and built-in procurement workflows.
