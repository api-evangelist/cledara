SaaS management is the practice of discovering, tracking, securing, and optimising every software subscription across your organisation. This definitive guide explains why SaaS management has become critical in 2026, with the average company managing 38 or more software vendors and wasting 25 to 30% of its budget on unused licences. It introduces the 5 Pillars of SaaS Management (discovery, spend management, compliance, procurement, and governance), clarifies how SaaS management differs from ITAM, FinOps, and IT operations, and provides a practical roadmap for getting started.
