What does a normal AI tool stack look like for a company with 30 to 500 employees? Based on Cledara platform data and industry benchmarks, roughly 80% of mid-market companies now pay for at least one AI tool, with an average of 4.1 AI subscriptions per company. AI accounts for approximately 6% of total SaaS spend, but that figure is growing faster than any other software category (108% year over year).
