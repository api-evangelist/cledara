Torii and Cledara are both strong SaaS management platforms, but they take fundamentally different approaches. Torii is built for IT security teams and focuses on discovering all SaaS applications in use, including shadow IT. Cledara is built for finance teams and focuses on controlling SaaS spending at the source using virtual cards.
