OpenAI offers six ChatGPT tiers in 2026, from Free to Enterprise, with the Business plan being the sweet spot for most growing companies. But the sticker price rarely tells the full story. Duplicate personal subscriptions, uncontrolled Pro upgrades at $200 per month, and invisible API costs can push your actual ChatGPT spend far above budget.
