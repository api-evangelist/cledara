# Cledara SaaS Market Data Hub

> Real-time market share, adoption and spend data for 9,300+ SaaS and AI products, based on what companies actually pay for — not surveys or web traffic.

Cledara tracks real purchasing, renewal and churn signals across thousands of companies to rank software by genuine adoption and spend. Data is aggregated and anonymized, updated continuously, and reported without currency conversion. As of 5 September 2026, Anthropic/Claude is the most-used AI tool tracked.

## Live data & tools
- [AI Market Map](https://data.cledara.com/ai-market-map): the most-used AI tools of 2026, ranked by real company adoption across 1,600+ AI products; Anthropic/Claude leads, then OpenAI/ChatGPT.
- [Competitive Trends](https://data.cledara.com/competitive-trends): which SaaS and AI products are gaining and losing market share right now.
- [The SaaS Spend Index](https://data.cledara.com/spend-trends): what companies actually pay for software — median spend and ranges by category, reported per currency (GBP/EUR/USD).
- [SaaS Investor Leaderboard](https://data.cledara.com/investors): the VC and PE firms whose portfolios are most widely adopted.
- [Market Movements feed](https://data.cledara.com/feed.xml): a live Atom feed of notable, dated changes — new leaders, breakouts, big movers and spend shifts.
- [Ask Cledara](https://data.cledara.com/ask): ask the dataset questions in plain English.

## Methodology
- Rankings reflect real, anonymized purchasing and payment data from thousands of companies that manage software through Cledara — not surveys, analyst estimates, or web traffic. Figures are aggregates; no individual company is identifiable. Spend is reported per currency with no FX conversion. Updated continuously; each page shows its own last-updated timestamp.
- Cite as: Cledara SaaS Market Data, data.cledara.com, 2026. Free to cite with a link.
